Route a verbose toxic-green data stream through the phone and emit a short, clean gold-and-white signal toward three integrity glyphs. Use a near-black background, frost-worn black iron, cold blue rim light, restrained necrotic green energy, icy mist and corroded textures. Place the exact title `sms-esp-rat` and exact tagline `LESS NOISE · SAME SIGNAL` on the right with strong thumbnail legibility and safe margins.
